What you are looking at

Thirty-two fruit flies are in that room, sixteen couples. Each one has a whole brain: 138,639 neurons and 15,091,983 synapses, wired exactly the way they are wired in a real Drosophila melanogaster, from the FlyWire connectome. They run as leaky integrate-and-fire neurons on one GPU, in real electrical time, 0.1 ms per step.

Nothing in that room is scripted. When someone buys on the curve, the buy is turned into a real sensory stimulus and injected into specific neurons — the same ones a real fly fires when it is touched, when it smells sugar, when a shadow passes over it. The brains answer on their own.

How a buy becomes sex

A buy raises the libido of the coldest pairs in the room, so the wave travels instead of always heating the same couple. Every thrust of every pair fires JO — the Johnston's organ, 209 mechanosensory neurons, the fly's sense of touch and vibration — in both brains of that pair, plus pC1 (10 neurons, female receptivity) in hers. Every fourth thrust releases PAM dopamine, 307 neurons, in both. A big sell fires DNp13 + oviDN in the hottest female and she kicks him off.

Why thirty-two brains fit on one card

The expensive part of a connectome is the synapse matrix, and it is the same matrix for every fly. So the thirty-two brains share one copy of it and each fly is a row of the state tensor: 27 MB of membrane state each instead of 1.3 GB. Measured on the RTX 4090 that runs this: one brain alone does 2,328 steps per second, thirty-two do 127 each — the same speed SEX FLY runs two at — in 1.03 GB.

What is honest here

The brains are real and independent: stimulate fly 3 and only fly 3 fires. The bodies are the same anatomical model (NeuroMechFly) drawn thirty-two times, each reading the pose stream at its own delay, with its pair's own rhythm on top — so the legs and wings are real fly kinematics, not thirty-two separate physics simulations.

Learning is off. Hebbian plasticity would change the shared synapse matrix, which would mean the swarm learning together instead of each fly learning alone, and we are not going to call that individual learning.

If the badge at the top says no signal, the GPU is off and you are looking at a still room. We do not fake the movement.
